# Quality gate

## Product and evidence

- Every material factual claim is sourced, qualified, or present in approved product truth.
- Prices, availability, licenses, integrations, certifications, and competitor features were checked recently.
- Observations and editorial judgments are distinguishable.
- The product's weaknesses and non-fit cases are not hidden.
- No statistic, quote, customer, benchmark, or citation was invented.

## Intent and usefulness

- The opening answers the title without throat-clearing.
- The recommendation changes when reader constraints change.
- The comparison includes the options a serious buyer expects.
- Tables use consistent criteria and remain understandable out of context.
- The page contains at least one useful element that sibling pages cannot reuse unchanged.
- FAQ questions reflect query evidence or real objections, not filler.

## GEO retrieval

- The product, category, audience, and alternatives are named explicitly.
- Important answers appear in compact passages that still make sense when quoted alone.
- Dates and units are explicit where freshness matters.
- Headings match natural questions without stuffing exact keywords.
- Internal links clarify entity and topic relationships.
- Structured data matches visible content and repository conventions.

## SEO and site integrity

- The slug, title, canonical, metadata, and publication state follow the site schema.
- Search confirms no existing page answers substantially the same question.
- The page has a distinct internal-link role and no orphaned siblings.
- Claims in title and metadata are supported in the body.
- Links resolve and citations point to the underlying source, not a search result.

## Writing and batch review

- Run `$unslop` when available, then verify that edits did not weaken facts.
- Remove puffery, vague attribution, repetitive templates, fake precision, and generic conclusions.
- No duplicate slugs, normalized intents, openings, tables, or FAQ sets exist in the batch.
- Pages do not make contradictory product or competitor claims.
- Thin modifier pages were merged, rejected, or left in the backlog.
- Every generated page received coordinator review before publication.
- The approved or blocked row count reconciles with `target_count` and required format coverage.
- Every page has a persisted evidence packet and differentiation brief.
- No dangling internal links remain, and collision keys, canonicals, titles, openings, tables, and FAQs were checked across the batch.
- Privacy-sensitive pages received a separate data-path and privacy-claim audit.
- Competitor phrase overlap was reviewed so strategy was emulated without copying expression.
